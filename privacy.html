Privacy Policy

CapiX — Capybara Arcade · Last updated: 27 September 2026

CapiX does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your device

High scores, the names you type for the leaderboard, CapiCash, owned skins and your settings are saved on your device. If iCloud is enabled, your local high scores are synced through your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage; the developer cannot access this data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Game Center leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy; the developer only sees the leaderboard entries that Game Center shows to every player.

Bug reports

The 🐞 button opens your Mail app with a pre-filled message that includes the app version, device model and iOS version. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email yourself. Emails are used only to answer you and fix problems.

Children

CapiX is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. Questions: giovanni.cicu@icloud.com.

Informativa sulla privacy

CapiX non raccoglie, conserva né condivide dati personali. Il gioco non ha account, pubblicità, analisi o tracciamento, e non contiene SDK di terze parti.

Record, nomi inseriti in classifica, CapiCash, skin e impostazioni restano sul tuo dispositivo; se iCloud è attivo, i record vengono sincronizzati tramite il tuo account iCloud, a cui lo sviluppatore non ha accesso. Se usi Game Center, i punteggi vengono inviati alle classifiche Game Center, gestite da Apple secondo la sua informativa. Il pulsante 🐞 apre l'app Mail con un messaggio precompilato (versione dell'app, modello del dispositivo, versione di iOS): non viene inviato nulla se non sei tu a spedirlo. Contatti: giovanni.cicu@icloud.com.
