CapiX — Capybara Arcade

Support

CapiX is an arcade game for iPhone and iPad: steer your orange out of the safe border, draw a line and close it to claim the board — while dodging the capybara and the sparx.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to giovanni.cicu@icloud.com. You can also use the 🐞 button inside the game: it prepares an email with your device and iOS version.

How to play

  • Swipe anywhere to steer the orange.
  • Leave the border to draw a trail, then return to the border to claim the enclosed area.
  • Don't let the capybara touch your trail, and dodge the sparx running along the border.
  • Reach the target percentage to clear the level. Cut a capybara off in a small area to squash it.
  • Time Attack: reach 60% before the clock runs out — the clock gets shorter every level.
  • 2 players (co-op or versus): the left half of the screen steers player 1, the right half player 2.

FAQ

How do I earn CapiCash? By clearing levels and squashing capybaras. Spend it in the Shop on skins, then choose them in the Editor. CapiCash cannot be bought with real money.

How do I unlock Special skins? They can't be bought: each one unlocks when you reach a goal shown on its card.

How do I turn off music or sounds? Tap ⚙ in the main menu (or Settings in the pause menu).

Are my scores online? If you're signed in to Game Center, your best scores appear on the Game Center leaderboards.
